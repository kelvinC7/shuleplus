Welcome to

ST MATTHEW'S SCHOOL

ST MATTHEW'S SCHOOL is a private school located in Kamembe Sector, Rusizi District, West Province. Established on February 1, 1980, the school provides a day learning environment designed to support students throughout their educational journey. The school offers education across multiple levels, including Nursery. ST MATTHEW'S SCHOOL serves learners across this level with programs aligned to national education standards.
