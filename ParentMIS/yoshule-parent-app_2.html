Parent App

Follow your child’s learning journey from anywhere. The yoShule parent app brings marks, attendance, homework, fees, ShuleCard pocket money and ShuleKit into one simple view for busy parents.

  • Start pageAny child’s code: pay fees, get the Babyeyi, top up the card
  • HomeYour linked child at a glance and what needs you
  • LearningMarks, report cards, attendance, homework and teacher feedback
  • PaySchool fees, ShuleCard top-ups and receipts
  • ShuleKitNext term’s kit: enrol, save, pay or use credit, track delivery
  • GoalsWeekly savings for ShuleKit, trips and more
  • ChatTeachers, circulars and school events
  • LanguagesEnglish, Ikinyarwanda, Français, Luganda, Português, Kiswahili, Ikirundi
